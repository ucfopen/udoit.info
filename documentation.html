About UDOIT

Originally designed for Webcourses@UCF, UDOIT now spans across many higher education institutions to provide ease in transitioning courses to adhering to modern accessibility specifications.

Built with accessibility in mind.

  • Thanks to UCFOpen, UDOIT is free and open source—but setup takes time and expertise, and hosting may incur ongoing costs.

    Check out more on our Github →

  • UDOIT has different installation methods, keeping the whole process flexible enough to tailor to your courses.

    View our Installation Guide to see all methods.

  • UDOIT plugs right into your Canvas course for seamless integration.
