What is UDOIT?

UDOIT is a free and open-source tool that educators can use to find and repair common accessibility issues in their online course content.

It integrates directly with the Canvas Learning Management System using the LTI standard, meaning that faculty and administrators can access it directly through Canvas without needing to leave the platform or log in separately.

What is Digital Accessibility?

Sometimes the way content is created is simple to navigate for some users, but difficult for others. With the influx of classes supporting online instruction, this means that some learners do not have access to certain online course material. Accessibility standards have been adopted to help ensure that web content meets criteria considering all users.

  • Visual Needs

    Learners with blindness, low vision, or color blindness.

    HOW UDOIT HELPS:

    UDOIT will scan courses for color contrast and ensure that images have text alternatives.

  • Auditory Needs

    Learners who are deaf or hard-of-hearing.

    HOW UDOIT HELPS:

    UDOIT will identify multimedia and help create and update captions and subtitles.

  • Motor Needs

    Users with physical disabilities that prevent them from using a mouse or keyboard.

    HOW UDOIT HELPS:

    UDOIT finds and helps fix visibility and other issues that prevent navigation with assitive technologies.

  • Cognitive Needs

    Users with cognitive and other hidden disabilities.

    HOW UDOIT HELPS:

    UDOIT makes sure that labels, links, and page landmarks are used correctly and with descriptive text.
