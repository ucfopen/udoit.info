What is UDOIT?

UDOIT is a free and open-source tool that educators can use to find and repair common accessibility issues in their online course content.

It integrates directly with the Canvas Learning Management System using the LTI standard, meaning that faculty and administrators can access it directly through Canvas without needing to leave the platform or log in separately.

What is Digital Accessibility?

Sometimes the way content is created is simple to navigate for some users, but difficult for others. Accessibility standards have been put in place to ensure websites meet criteria considering all users, eliminating content barriers.

With the influx of classes supporting online instruction, it is important to follow guidelines supporting visual and screen reader functionality to support students with needs. Digital accessibility is the gateway to people with disabilities having equal access to online material.

  • Visual Needs

    Users with blindness, low vision, or color blindness rely on structured, well-contrasted content.

    HOW UDOIT HELPS:

    UDOIT will scan courses for colour contrast and ensure that content is accessible.

  • Auditory Needs

    Users relying on screen readers need cleanly formatted and descriptive content to navigate smoothly.

    HOW UDOIT HELPS:

    UDOIT will identify files, pages, or links with nondescript titles, or issues that may cause difficulty.

  • Cognitive Needs

    Users with hidden disabilities may have trouble navigating if course styles override their own settings.

    HOW UDOIT HELPS:

    UDOIT has multiple built-in tools to address a range of violations that may cause users to struggle with navigation and interpretation of online course material.

  • Other

    Some users may need extra accommodations for content that is published online.

    HOW UDOIT HELPS:

    UDOIT allows faculty to adjust file names, video captions, and other materials that may be present in courses.
